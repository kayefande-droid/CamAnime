Watch without fighting the internet

Adaptive video quality

Pick Auto and HLS adapts to weak links, or choose the provider's own highest/lowest level. MP4 plays at the resolution the provider published — never a simulated one.

Data saver

On slow or metered networks CamAnime trims effects, swaps to light posters and enlarges buffering so episodes never stutter.

English sub & English dub

Every episode page tracks available English SUB and English DUB sources separately, with sensible defaults and a choice you make once.

Skip where supported

Speed controls, resume positions, autoplay next episode and watch progress saved on this device or to your account.

4 layout templates

Reduce motion
