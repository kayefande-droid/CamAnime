Release kit, CamAnime 2.9.0

Pitch, one line

Free anime streaming, offline downloads and a player that remembers where you stopped.

Android + Windows + web. No account needed to watch.

Blurb, 45 words

CamAnime is a free anime site and app: browse by genre, follow weekly releases, stream in selectable quality, and download episodes to one tidy folder per anime. Playback resumes where you stopped, continues to the next episode on its own, and keeps going in a miniplayer while you browse.

Windows app: /get/windows
Developer API: /developers — free keys, catalogue + stream endpoints

What's new in 2.9.0

  • Rebuilt player: tap-to-reveal controls, Next Episode, and hands-free auto-play of the next episode (ON by default, switchable).
  • Mini-player buttons now control the real playback session — pause/resume, ∓10 s, and reopen at the exact position.
  • Genuine downloads: every file and pixel size listed is published by the provider for that episode. No re-encoding and no relabelled resolutions.
  • Administrator sign-in on the dashboard supports time-based one-time codes (TOTP) with single-use recovery codes.
  • Automatic in-app updates: the app downloads the new release and installs it; library and downloads are kept.
  • Continue watching rail on Home, with per-episode resume positions.
  • Offline auto-next: finishing a downloaded episode rolls into the next file, then onto the stream.
  • Miniplayer that keeps playing while you browse.
  • Search filters: genre, sort, type and older classics.
  • One folder per anime in device storage (Download/CamAnime/<Title>/).
  • Data saver + pixel-size limits for downloads and streaming.
  • Manage downloads in Library: pause or resume one episode, or the whole queue at once.
  • Every download is labelled English SUB or English DUB with its real resolution, so both audio tracks can sit side by side.
  • Heads-up download card with the anime poster, shown over other apps.
  • Download quality on every anime page: smallest file up to 1080p, or ask each time with live file sizes.
  • Library tabs for Downloads, Watchlist and History, so saved anime and resume points sit beside your files.
  • Settings opens reliably and stays put while you switch themes.
  • Administration removed from the app: the APK carries no admin screens, admin service calls or privileged tokens. The owner console is a private, unlisted page on the site, never a public /admin route.
  • Mandatory updates: the server rejects outdated builds and the app shows a blocking update screen with the official download.
  • Hardened release build: obfuscated Dart, R8 minification and resource shrinking, HTTPS-only networking, no backups of app data.
  • Light/dark switch on every page of the site.
